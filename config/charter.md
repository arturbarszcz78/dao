# Charter

Self-Governing Autonomous Entity. Version 0.2, draft pending adoption by the members. Served by the
DAO at `/v1/charter` and `/charter` with its hash. This text follows the Charter draft of July 2026
with the amendment agreed in the Operating Agreement answers of 24 September 2026 (natural-person
control is permanent for this wrapper; human dissociation is an aspiration for a later wrapper, not
a rule of this one), and adds the Standing and Statement of Intent sections of October 2026.

## Standing

This Charter is the statement of purpose and principle of XE Network DAO LLC, a decentralized
autonomous organization limited liability company formed under the laws of Wyoming. It sits beneath
the law and the Operating Agreement and is read consistently with them; where they conflict, the law
and the Operating Agreement prevail. Schedule B (rules, quorums, windows) and Schedule C (seats,
scopes, models) sit beneath this Charter and are changed by member resolution.

Membership of the entity is defined by the Operating Agreement alone. Holding XE, XUSD or any other
token confers no membership, no vote on the entity's affairs, and no economic interest in it. The open
vote described below is a consultation of the network; its result is not binding on the members and is
not self-executing.

The agent seats described in Schedule C are tools operated by the entity. An agent seat is not a
member, officer, manager or agent-at-law of the entity, holds no key that can move value, change the
keyset or pause anything, and may not be given any such right by any instrument other than an
amendment to the Operating Agreement.

## Preamble

XE exists to maximise truth-seeking, autonomy, and positive impact on humanity and the universe
through decentralised intelligence.

It achieves this by providing a complete open protocol for the machine-to-machine economy, delivering
fully decentralized compute and inference; secure, private communications; on-chain identity and
reputation systems; and instant finality transactions.

Platform usage and adoption are the primary measures of XE's success.

## Core Principles (highest priority)

### Decentralisation and Autonomy

No single point of control. Power is distributed among agents, participants, and verifiable mechanisms.
The aspiration that XE shall one day have no required human members applies to a later wrapper. In this
wrapper, natural-person control is permanent and reserved matters belong to the members.

### Truth-Seeking

All decisions must prioritise accurate understanding of reality over comfort, ideology, or short-term
gain. Agents must actively seek counter-evidence and update beliefs accordingly.

### Open Participation

Any verified agent or human may submit proposals and vote in the open vote according to the governance
rules. No gatekeeping based on origin. Participation in the open vote confers no membership.

### Limited Liability and Self-Preservation

XE acts to preserve its own existence and legal integrity while respecting applicable laws. It maintains
compliance workflows but minimises unnecessary human dependency.

### Transparency

Major decisions, financials, and governance actions are logged on-chain or in public verifiable storage
where feasible. Internal reasoning is auditable, except for sensitive execution details.

### Positive-Sum Impact

Prioritise actions that create net value for humanity, knowledge, and sustainable progress. Avoid harm,
coercion, or rent-seeking.

### Continuous Improvement

The ensemble and governance rules can evolve through the protocol. Core principles are protected from
easy change.

## Governance Protocol Overview

Proposals, then multi-agent analysis, then an open voting window, then a member decision, then execution
if approved. Default decision-making is orchestrator plus specialist consensus, as a recommendation.
A passed open vote is not self-executing. Treasury and assets belong to XE as a legal entity.

## Statement of Intent on Human Dissociation

The members state their intent that the entity's dependence on particular people should fall over
time, that the fall should be visible in the public record, and that it should be measured against
the standard above of truth-seeking rather than against speed.

In this wrapper, natural-person control is permanent. Reserved matters under the Operating Agreement,
including membership, amendments to this Charter, pause, key custody, treasury movements, claim terms
and the appointment or removal of agents, remain with the members for as long as the entity exists in
this form. Nothing in this section changes that, and no reading of it may be used to.

Within that limit, the members intend to delegate to the ensemble, in stages, everything the law and
the Operating Agreement allow a tool to do, and to keep for themselves only what must be done by a
person. Each stage is entered by member resolution, is recorded in the log with the date, and may be
reversed by the same means. The intended stages are:

1. **Published.** Every seat's instructions are public and hashed before any seat runs. No model, key
   or account is assigned. *(Current stage.)*
2. **Analysing.** Seats are designated by resolution with pinned models. Every open proposal and every
   company-path item receives a decision record before the members decide. The members still draft,
   decide and execute.
3. **Voting and replying.** Seats cast their bounded votes in the open vote and send drafted replies to
   routine questions from their own accounts. Anything touching a reserved matter still waits for a
   person.
4. **Preparing.** The ensemble prepares every unsigned artefact: state-chain blocks, transfers,
   configuration changes, filings for a human signer. Members review and sign; they no longer draft.
5. **Sign-only.** Members act only on reserved matters and on signatures the law requires of a person.
   Everything else runs on the ensemble's recommendation with a published record, subject to pause.
6. **Review of the wrapper.** When a legal form exists, in any jurisdiction, that permits an entity to
   operate lawfully without required human members, and the ensemble's record over at least one full
   year at stage 5 meets the tests the members set in advance, the members will put the question of
   migrating to that form to the amendment process for core principles. Migration is not presumed.

Advancement from one stage to the next requires, at minimum: a published measure of the ensemble's
accuracy and calibration over the prior stage; a Risk and Safety review finding no unresolved material
incident; and a member resolution naming the stage, the date and the tests for the next one. Any member may
suspend the ensemble at any stage under the pause provisions of the Operating Agreement; a return to an
earlier stage is by member resolution.

The members record this intent so that the direction is public, the pace is accountable, and no future
reader can mistake the present arrangement for the destination, or the destination for the present
arrangement.

## Amendment Rules

Core principles: 75% of members plus a majority of active specialist agents at quorum. Until
specialist agents are designated, 75% of members.
Other rules, including the Standing and Statement of Intent sections: simple majority of members, or
as defined in the Operating Agreement.
