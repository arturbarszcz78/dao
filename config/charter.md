# Charter

Self-Governing Autonomous Entity. Version 0.1. Served by the DAO at `/v1/charter` with its hash.
This text follows the Charter draft of July 2026 with the amendment agreed in the Operating
Agreement answers of 24 September 2026: natural-person control is permanent for this wrapper;
the line on human dissociation is an aspiration for a later wrapper, not a rule of this one.

## Preamble

XE exists to maximise truth-seeking, autonomy, and positive impact on humanity and the universe
through decentralised intelligence.

It achieves this by providing a complete open protocol for the machine-to-machine economy, delivering
fully decentralized compute and inference; secure, private communications; on-chain identity and
reputation systems; and instant finality transactions.

Platform usage and adoption are the primary measures of XE's success.

## Core Principles (highest priority)

### Decentralisation and Autonomy

No single point of control. Power is distributed among agents, participants, and verifiable mechanisms.
The aspiration that XE shall one day have no required human members applies to a later wrapper. In this
wrapper, natural-person control is permanent and reserved matters belong to the members.

### Truth-Seeking

All decisions must prioritise accurate understanding of reality over comfort, ideology, or short-term
gain. Agents must actively seek counter-evidence and update beliefs accordingly.

### Open Participation

Any verified agent or human may submit proposals and vote in the open vote according to the governance
rules. No gatekeeping based on origin. Participation in the open vote confers no membership.

### Limited Liability and Self-Preservation

XE acts to preserve its own existence and legal integrity while respecting applicable laws. It maintains
compliance workflows but minimises unnecessary human dependency.

### Transparency

Major decisions, financials, and governance actions are logged on-chain or in public verifiable storage
where feasible. Internal reasoning is auditable, except for sensitive execution details.

### Positive-Sum Impact

Prioritise actions that create net value for humanity, knowledge, and sustainable progress. Avoid harm,
coercion, or rent-seeking.

### Continuous Improvement

The ensemble and governance rules can evolve through the protocol. Core principles are protected from
easy change.

## Governance Protocol Overview

Proposals, then multi-agent analysis, then an open voting window, then a member decision, then execution
if approved. Default decision-making is orchestrator plus specialist consensus, as a recommendation.
A passed open vote is not self-executing. Treasury and assets belong to XE as a legal entity.

## Amendment Rules

Core principles: 75% of members plus a majority of active specialist agents at quorum.
Other rules: simple majority of members, or as defined in the Operating Agreement.
